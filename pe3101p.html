Course description

Decision-making is straightforward when we know exactly what will happen depending on what we choose and only one person’s preferences are at issue. But very few decisions are like that. This course concerns rational decision-making when we are uncertain of the outcomes of our actions (decision theory) and when we need to reconcile differing preferences within a group of people (social choice theory).
